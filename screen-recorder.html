Privacy Policy for Screen Recorder Master

Effective Date: October 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Recorder Master ("we", "our") handles user information and permissions.

1. No Personal Data Collected

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number).

2. Media & Local Storage

All video recordings, audio tracks, and screenshots created with the app are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not transmit or upload your recorded video content to external servers.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Screen Capture (Media Projection): Required solely to record your device screen during recording sessions.
  • Microphone / Record Audio: Used to capture voice commentary and gameplay audio upon your request.
  • Storage / Media: Required to save your recorded video files to your device gallery.
  • Floating Window (Overlay): Used to provide floating quick-record and pause buttons over other apps.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The application uses verified industry-standard third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Serves non-personalized or personalized advertising. May collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) and anonymous diagnostic signals.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Used to configure dynamic app settings remotely for stability.
  • RevenueCat: Manages optional in-app purchases and subscriptions securely through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or financial information.

5. Data Deletion

Since we do not store personal profiles on remote servers, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
